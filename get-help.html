Getting Professional Help

While self-care strategies and calming activities are important tools, sometimes anxiety requires additional support. Reaching out to trained professionals or using mental health resources can provide the guidance, tools, and reassurance you need.

Emergency?

If you are in immediate danger or thinking of harming yourself, call 911 (in the U.S.) or your local emergency number right away.

Resources

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

    Call or text 988 for free, confidential support 24/7.

  • SAMHSA National Helpline

    1-800-662-HELP (4357) for treatment referrals and information.

  • Make It OK

    A campaign to reduce stigma and encourage conversations about mental health.

Online Therapy Services

  • BetterHelp

    Connect with licensed therapists online.

  • Talkspace

    Therapy via text, audio, or video.

  • Brightside

    Online therapy and psychiatry for anxiety and depression.
