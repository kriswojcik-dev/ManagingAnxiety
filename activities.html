Activities for Managing Anxiety

Activities are powerful tools to manage anxiety by providing focus, relaxation, and a sense of accomplishment. Explore these resources and practices to discover what works best for you.

Suggested Activities

Try Journaling to process your thoughts and reduce anxiety.

Engage in Adult Coloring to relax and boost creativity.

Play Cozy Games like Animal Crossing for comfort and calm.

Take a walk in Nature with the help of AllTrails hiking maps.

Practice the 3-3-3 Rule to quickly ground yourself.

Journaling for Anxiety

Set Yourself Up for Success

  • Use whichever format works best for you...
  • If it makes you more interested in writing, decorate or personalize your journal...
  • Set a goal to write for a certain amount of time each day.
  • Decide ahead of time when and/or where you will write each day.
  • Consider what makes you want to write in the first place...

The Five Steps to WRITE (Adams, n.d.)

  • W – What do you want to write about? Name it.
  • R – Review or reflect on your topic...
  • I – Investigate your thoughts and feelings...
  • T – Time yourself...
  • E – Exit “smart” by re-reading what you’ve written...

Keep in Mind (Howes, 2011)

  • It’s okay to write only a few words...
  • Don’t worry about what to write about...
  • Don’t worry about how well you write...
  • Remember that no-one else needs to read what you’ve written...

Cozy Games for Anxiety

  • Offer comforting routines and gentle progress.
  • Create safe, welcoming spaces to unwind.
